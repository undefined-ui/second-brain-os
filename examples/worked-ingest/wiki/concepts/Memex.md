# Memex

## In one paragraph

The Memex is [[Vannevar Bush]]'s 1945 proposal for a personal, curated knowledge store in which documents are tied together by associative trails that the owner builds and follows. The connections between documents were meant to be as valuable as the documents themselves, and the store was private and actively curated rather than public and passively indexed.

## Where it came from

The only source for the Memex in this vault is [[LLM Wiki (Karpathy)]], which cites it as the idea closest in spirit to the [[LLM wiki]] pattern. Bush's original writing is not covered by any source here, so the description above is limited to what that source attributes to him.

## What supports it

The source's reading is that Bush's vision was closer to a private, curated, link-centred store than to what the web became, and that an LLM wiki realises it.

## What argues against it

Nothing in this vault argues against the Memex as an idea. The source identifies its unsolved problem: who does the maintenance of the trails. Its claim is that an LLM agent is the missing maintainer.

## Open questions

What Bush actually proposed beyond the summary in the source, and whether the trail model maps onto wikilinks between pages or onto something closer to saved paths through a wiki.
