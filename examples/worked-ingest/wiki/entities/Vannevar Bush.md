# Vannevar Bush

## What it is

The originator of the [[Memex]] proposal of 1945. Nothing else about him is covered by any source in this vault.

## Why it appears here

[[LLM Wiki (Karpathy)]] cites his Memex as the closest ancestor of the [[LLM wiki]] pattern: a private, curated store where the links between documents matter as much as the documents, missing only someone to do the maintenance.
