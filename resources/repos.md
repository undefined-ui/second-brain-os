# Repositories

Open-source implementations of the pattern. Reading other people's page
contracts and prompts is the fastest way to improve your own.

- **[claude-obsidian](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-obsidian)** by AgriciDaniel. A self-organizing second brain on the
  Karpathy wiki pattern. Scaffolds the vault, sets up the connection, and ships
  presets for different roles: executive, builder, creator, researcher.
- **[obsidian-second-brain](https://github.com/eugeniughelbur/obsidian-second-brain)** by eugeniughelbur. Around 43 ready commands such as
  `/obsidian-save`, `/obsidian-daily` and `/obsidian-find`. Works across Claude,
  Codex and Gemini, so you are not locked to one agent.
- **[second-brain-starter](https://github.com/coleam00/second-brain-starter)** by coleam00. Interviews you, generates a plan for a
  proactive assistant, then builds it from plain text files, Python and an
  Obsidian vault.
- **[second-brain](https://github.com/NicholasSpisak/second-brain)** by NicholasSpisak. Skills for ingest, lint and query,
  installable through npm, close to the original gist.

All of them are text files and scripts. Clone one and reshape it, or take the
parts you like and keep your own structure.

Karpathy's original [`llm-wiki` gist](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) is the source document for all of these. It
was published on 2026-04-04 and describes the pattern at the level of intent
rather than implementation, which is why every repo above fills the gaps
differently.
