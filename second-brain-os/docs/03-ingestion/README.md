# Ingestion

Getting everything you read, watch and write into `raw/` with as little
friction as possible, then into the wiki without flooding it.

Capture is the part that decides whether the vault survives. Every step between
seeing something and having it saved is a step where you will stop doing it, so
the goal is one click or one command, always the same one.

The second half matters as much: what you feed the vault sets its ceiling. A
hundred clipped listicles produce a hundred thin pages and a graph that says
nothing.
