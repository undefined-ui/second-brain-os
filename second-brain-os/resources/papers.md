# Papers

Research relevant to how a knowledge base retrieves and reasons. Read these if
you want to understand why the wiki approach works, or to decide whether you
need a vector database at all.

- **From Local to Global: A Graph RAG Approach to Query-Focused
  Summarization** (Microsoft Research). Introduces GraphRAG: build an entity
  graph from a corpus, summarise communities, answer global questions the
  chunk-retrieval approach cannot.
- **HippoRAG**. Memory-inspired retrieval using a knowledge graph and
  personalised PageRank, aimed at multi-hop questions.
- **Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts**. Why dumping a
  whole vault into context degrades answers, and the argument for structured
  retrieval over long-context stuffing.
