# What good looks like

Concrete criteria, so you can tell whether yours is working rather than guessing.

## The test

Ask it something you half-remember reading about, months ago, that you cannot
name precisely.

If it comes back with the idea, the sources, and two related things you had
forgotten, it works. If it returns nothing, or returns a general explanation that
could have come from any model, it does not.

## Signals of health

**You reach for it before searching the web.** The strongest signal, and it
happens on its own once the vault is dense enough.

**Answers cite pages you can open,** and the pages say what the answer said.

**The graph has clusters you did not plan.** Evidence it is tracking what you
actually pay attention to rather than what you told it to.

**New sources mostly update existing pages** rather than creating new ones. That
means the vault has the concepts already and is deepening rather than sprawling.

**It contradicts you occasionally,** because you have material on both sides of
something.

## Signals of trouble

**Orphan rate climbing.** Ingestion has stopped linking. See
[metrics](../05-graphs/metrics.md).

**Pages that read as summaries** rather than explanations. The page contract in
`CLAUDE.md` needs tightening.

**Concept pages untouched for months while sources keep arriving.** New material
is landing in new pages instead of updating existing ones, which produces four
pages about one thing.

**You stopped opening it.** The honest one. Usually means capture broke, not that
the idea failed.

## A realistic timeline

**Week one.** Setup, ten or twenty sources. Feels like a folder with extra steps.
This is normal and it is where most people quit.

**Week two to four.** Fifty to a hundred sources. First moment of the vault
connecting two things you read weeks apart. This is when it starts being worth
the setup.

**Month three.** A few hundred pages. It answers questions faster than you could
by rereading, and it surfaces things you had forgotten saving.

**Month six.** It knows things you do not remember learning. That is the state
everyone describes as the payoff, and it is real, and it takes six months of
feeding it.
