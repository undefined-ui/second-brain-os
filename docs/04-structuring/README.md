# Structuring

The rules that decide whether your vault becomes a graph or a landfill.

Ingestion determines what goes in. Structure determines whether any of it comes
back out. Two vaults holding the same thousand sources can be a research
partner or an unsearchable pile, and the difference is entirely in the rules
below.

Everything here belongs in your `CLAUDE.md`, because a rule the agent reads once
in a chat lasts one session, and a rule in `CLAUDE.md` applies to every page it
ever writes.
